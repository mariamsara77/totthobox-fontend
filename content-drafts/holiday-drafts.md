# Holiday Record Editorial Draft Template

Target Word Count: 450–700 words.

### Required Structure
1. **Title & Date Information**:
   - Official name in Bengali and English.
   - Fixed Gregorian date, Hijri/Bengali date (if applicable).
   - Legal classification: সাধারণ ছুটি (General Holiday) / নির্বাহী আদেশে ছুটি (Executive Order) / ঐচ্ছিক ছুটি (Optional).
2. **Historical Background & Significance**:
   - Why is this day observed in Bangladesh?
   - The historical or religious milestone commemorated.
3. **Government & Public Observance**:
   - How government offices, educational institutions, and financial organizations observe this holiday.
   - Official protocol (e.g., national flag hoisting, wreath laying, public prayer).
4. **Public Advisory & Practical Utility**:
   - Status of public transport, hospitals, emergency services.
   - Long weekend planning tips if connected with Friday/Saturday.
