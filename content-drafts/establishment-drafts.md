# Establishment Record Editorial Draft Template

Target Word Count: 500–800 words.

### Required Structure
1. **Overview & Identity**:
   - Official title in Bengali and English.
   - Exact geographic location: Division, District, Upazila/Thana, Union/Ward.
   - Classification: প্রশাসনিক (Administrative) / শিক্ষা (Educational) / স্বাস্থ্য (Healthcare) / প্রত্নতাত্ত্বিক (Heritage).
2. **Architecture & History**:
   - Year or era of foundation.
   - Architect / patron / historical builder.
   - Distinctive architectural features and campus scale.
3. **Public Services & Citizen Charter**:
   - Primary civic services provided to citizens.
   - Official working hours, visiting schedules, ticket fees (if applicable).
   - Citizen charter highlights.
4. **Verified Contact Channels**:
   - Official helpline / reception telephone number.
   - Physical address and landmark directions.
   - Verified official website or portal link (bangladesh.gov.bd).
