# Public Figure Profile Editorial Draft Template

Target Word Count: 500–800 words.

### Required Structure
1. **Name & Identity**:
   - Full name, honorific, and public title.
   - Date and place of birth (and demise, if applicable).
   - Primary domain: সাহিত্য (Literature) / বিজ্ঞান (Science) / রাজনীতি (Politics) / শিল্প (Arts) / সমাজসেবা (Social Reform).
2. **Early Life & Education**:
   - Academic institutions attended, mentors, and early formative influences.
3. **Major Contributions & Works**:
   - Publications, discoveries, inventions, or administrative reforms.
   - National and international recognition, awards (স্বাধীনতা পদক, একুশে পদক, Nobel, etc.).
4. **Historical Significance**:
   - Enduring impact on Bangladeshi society and future generations.
   - Verified biographical sources and archival citations.
