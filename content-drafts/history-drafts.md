# History Record Editorial Draft Template

Target Word Count: 500–850 words.

### Required Structure
1. **Event / Epoch Name**:
   - Exact historical terminology.
   - Time period / century / calendar year.
   - Geographic epicenter in Bengal / Bangladesh.
2. **Context & Catalyst**:
   - Political, social, or economic conditions preceding the event.
   - Primary catalysts and key figures involved.
3. **Chronological Unfolding**:
   - Step-by-step progression of the historical event.
   - Key battles, treaties, declarations, or demonstrations.
4. **Impact & Modern Legacy**:
   - How this event shaped contemporary Bangladeshi identity, laws, or culture.
   - Commemorative monuments, museums, or state holidays associated with the event.
   - Primary reference books and archaeological citations.
